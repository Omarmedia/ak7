FAQ about this tour..

What does the tour include?

The tour includes a luxury car with a panoramic glass roof, a professional guide and photographer, thermal suits, Arctic snacks, warm drinks, free tripod rental, professional photos, and hotel pickup and drop-off.

What are the optional experiences?

On clear nights, you can enjoy stargazing with a telescope. There's also the opportunity to go snowshoeing to remote, quiet places to enhance your Northern Lights experience.

Where does the tour take place?

The journey starts in Tromsø. Based on weather conditions, the guide may take you along fjords, into valleys, or across the border to Finland to maximize the chances of seeing the Northern Lights.

Who should not join this tour?

This activity is not suitable for children under 7, people over 95, wheelchair users, or those with claustrophobia.

What should I bring?

You should bring warm clothing and warm shoes to ensure comfort during the tour.

Are the Northern Lights guaranteed?

The Northern Lights are a natural phenomenon, so sightings cannot be guaranteed. The team will do their best to take you where the chances are highest.
