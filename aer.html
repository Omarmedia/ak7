FAQ about this tour..

What does the tour include?

Round-trip transportation by minibus/minivan or electric vehicle from Tromsø; reindeer feeding; stories and lessons about Sami culture; campfire, hot drinks, and Sámi snacks; lasso throwing (weather permitting).

What are the highlights?

You'll travel through the Arctic landscape until you reach the Sámi Camp. You'll be able to feed and pet the reindeer and take pictures with them and the community. You'll hear Sámi stories while enjoying their traditional food and drink.

Where is the meeting point?

Go to the main entrance of the Magic Ice Bar, where your guide will meet you to board the minibus/minivan or the electric vehicle. The tour visits Ramfjordbotn and Skavskogen and returns to Magic Ice Bar Tromsø.

Who should not join this tour?

Suitable for all ages 5 and up. Not suitable for children under 4 years.

What should I bring?

Warm clothing, camera, and daypack. Thermal or wool top, comfortable walking shoes and boots suitable for snow, gloves, and wool hat.

What is not allowed?

Baby strollers; luggage or large bags; drinks in the vehicle; non-folding wheelchairs; alcohol and drugs; electric wheelchairs; explosive substances; alcoholic drinks in the vehicle; food and drinks in the vehicle.
