FAQ about this tour..

What does the tour include?

  • Traditional Norwegian meal
  • Warm drinks
  • Transportation by minibus
  • Local guide
  • Professional photography

What are the optional experiences?

  • Immerse yourself in the natural beauty of Kvaløya and Sommarøy
  • Capture the magic of the Arctic landscape with help from your guide
  • Enjoy a traditional Norwegian meal and warm drinks at a scenic spot
  • Look out for Arctic wildlife such as reindeer and sea eagles
  • Admire rugged mountains, icy beaches, and picturesque fishing villages

Where does the tour take place?

Leave Tromsø behind and cross the bridge to Kvaløya, where city life fades into wild Arctic nature. The road takes you through dramatic mountains, scenic fjords, and quiet coastal villages, with plenty of photo stops along the way to enjoy breathtaking views.

As you approach Sommarøy, the scenery becomes almost surreal – with white coral beaches, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and scattered islands stretching toward the horizon.

Your guide will help you capture the magic, both with your own camera and by taking professional photos for you throughout the journey. Wildlife such as reindeer, moose, and sea eagles may be spotted.

At a specially chosen viewpoint, you will enjoy a traditional Norwegian meal and warm drinks before returning to Tromsø.

Who should not join this tour?

  • Children under 6 years
  • Wheelchair users

What should I bring?

  • Warm clothing
  • Swimwear
  • Towel
  • Drinking water

Are the Northern Lights guaranteed?

Be prepared for cold weather and dress accordingly. Keep an eye out for wildlife, including reindeer and sea eagles. Important: If the minimum number of participants is not reached, the tour will be canceled.

Please note: The Northern Lights are a natural phenomenon and sightings can never be guaranteed.
